Vettr Chrome Extension — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Vettr (“we”) provides a Chrome extension to help business buyers aggregate listings, analyze deals, and save opportunities. This policy describes what the extension stores and sends.

Data stored locally on your device

  • Saved deals, notes, calculator inputs, buy box settings, and data-source configuration
  • Your Vettr sign-in session token after you log in (used only to sync My Deals with your Vettr account)

Data sent to Vettr servers

When you sign in, the extension may send saved deals and related notes/status to the Vettr API so they appear in your account on vettr.pages.dev. We do not sell your data.

Third-party services

  • Google Sheets API — only if you connect a private Google Sheet as a data source (OAuth via Chrome identity)
  • Public listing sites & sheet URLs you configure — fetched in your browser to import deals you choose

Permissions

  • Storage — save deals and preferences locally
  • Active tab / host access — show the deal calculator on listing pages and read page data you analyze
  • Identity — optional Google sign-in for private spreadsheets
  • Notifications — optional alerts when new deals match your buy box

Contact

Questions: use the support contact on the Vettr website or your account settings.

Changes

We may update this policy; the extension version and this page will reflect material changes.
